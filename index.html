01 · Introduction

What is SHILPA?

SHILPA (Supportive Holistic Inclusive Learning Platform Application) is a research initiative focused on designing and developing a mobile-first learning platform that caters to the diverse needs of children with disabilities in Sri Lanka and beyond.

The platform integrates multiple adaptive technologies including text-to-speech, sign language support, eye-tracking input, and simplified cognitive interfaces — all within a single, unified application accessible to every learner, regardless of ability.

Our research aims to bridge the accessibility gap in digital education by providing a holistic solution that supports four key impairment categories through evidence-based design methodologies and user-centered testing.

4 Impairment categories addressed
15% Global population with disability
240M Children with disabilities worldwide
1 Unified inclusive learning platform
02 · Domain

Research Domain

Our research is grounded in accessibility computing, mobile HCI, and inclusive education technology, exploring a multidisciplinary landscape of challenges and opportunities.

Background

Digital learning technology has expanded rapidly, yet children with disabilities remain significantly underserved. Existing applications address individual impairments in isolation, creating fragmented experiences and barriers to holistic education. Sri Lanka's special education sector faces compounded challenges — limited digital resources, lack of localized tools, and insufficient teacher training in assistive technologies. SHILPA addresses this critical gap by unifying solutions under one platform, grounded in Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles.

Research Gap

Despite growing assistive technology research, existing work lacks:

  • Unified platforms supporting multiple disability types simultaneously
  • Localized content for South Asian educational contexts
  • Adaptive AI-driven personalization for diverse learning needs
  • Evidence-based usability testing with children with disabilities
  • Parent and educator integration in the learning loop

Research Problems

Core problems driving this research:

  • Inaccessible UI/UX in mainstream educational apps for children with impairments
  • Lack of multi-modal input (voice, gesture, gaze, touch) in learning platforms
  • Absence of adaptive difficulty scaling aligned to cognitive ability
  • No consolidated progress tracking across different disability needs

Research Objectives

  • Design and develop a cross-disability inclusive mobile learning platform for children aged 4–14
  • Implement screen-reader compatibility, sign language animations, and alternative input modalities
  • Evaluate usability and learning outcomes through controlled user testing with target populations
  • Develop an adaptive algorithm that personalizes content delivery based on individual learning profiles
  • Validate the platform's effectiveness through pre/post assessments and qualitative feedback
  • Provide recommendations for deploying inclusive EdTech in low-resource school environments

04 · What We Provide

What SHILPA Offers

A comprehensive set of adaptive tools and modules designed to deliver equitable, personalized learning to every child.

Screen Reader & TTS

Full screen reader integration with text-to-speech in Sinhala, Tamil, and English to support learners with visual impairments throughout every activity.

Sign Language Animations

Animated sign language avatars for Sinhala Sign Language providing visual translations of all learning content for children with hearing impairments.

Switch & Eye-Gaze Input

Alternative input methods including switch access and eye-gaze tracking allow learners with physical impairments to navigate and interact without traditional touch.

Adaptive Learning Engine

AI-powered personalization adjusts content difficulty, pacing, and modality based on each learner's performance profile and cognitive capacity.

Simplified Cognitive UI

High-contrast, symbol-based interfaces with minimal text and clear visual cues designed specifically for learners with cognitive and intellectual disabilities.

Parent & Educator Dashboard

Real-time progress tracking, activity reports, and curriculum management tools empowering parents and teachers to support each child's learning journey.
